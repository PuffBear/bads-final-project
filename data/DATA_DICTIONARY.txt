DATA DICTIONARY AND LINK KEYS

customers.csv
customer_id, signup_date, age, gender, region, city, annual_income_band_inr, preferred_category, orders_before_2026, spend_before_2026_inr, days_since_last_order_at_2025_12_31, website_visits_dec_2025, email_engagement_dec_2025, marketing_consent

campaign_catalog.csv
campaign_id, campaign_name, channel, featured_category, start_date, end_date, discount_pct, campaign_objective

campaign_exposures.csv
exposure_id, customer_id, campaign_id, exposure_date, channel, delivered, opened, clicked, contact_cost_inr, purchased_within_14_days

orders.csv
order_id, customer_id, order_date, attributed_exposure_id, gross_merchandise_value_inr, discount_inr, shipping_fee_inr, order_total_inr, payment_mode

order_items.csv
order_item_id, order_id, product_id, quantity, unit_price_inr, line_gross_value_inr

product_catalog.csv
product_id, product_name, category, list_price_inr

weekly_channel_performance.csv
week_start_date, channel, ad_spend_inr, impressions, clicks, conversions, attributed_revenue_inr

brand_perception_survey.csv
respondent_id, customer_id, brand, perceived_quality_1_to_5, perceived_affordability_1_to_5, perceived_innovation_1_to_5, perceived_naturalness_1_to_5, perceived_trendiness_1_to_5

RELATIONSHIPS
customers.customer_id = campaign_exposures.customer_id = orders.customer_id = brand_perception_survey.customer_id
campaign_catalog.campaign_id = campaign_exposures.campaign_id
orders.attributed_exposure_id = campaign_exposures.exposure_id (when nonblank)
orders.order_id = order_items.order_id
product_catalog.product_id = order_items.product_id
weekly_channel_performance.channel = campaign_catalog.channel (channel labels only; aggregates are separate)

KEY INTERPRETATION
purchased_within_14_days = 1 if exposure is associated with a purchase during the subsequent 14 days; otherwise 0. Pre-2026 behaviour fields are fixed before the campaign start. Delivered, opened, clicked are observed campaign-event fields rather than pre-contact customer attributes.
